About This Project

I am a high school student who writes independent research papers to understand large questions from the ground up. This project grew out of a survey I wrote on life-extending medicine, and the pieces here are the pillars that survey rests on. Cardiovascular disease, neurodegeneration, and cancer are the failure modes that decide how long most people live.

I built this site to practice something medicine actually requires, which is explaining hard science to the people it affects without either frightening them or making it smaller than it is. If a page here makes a difficult idea clearer without misleading you, it has done its job.

Every clinical claim is cited to a primary source. I am not a licensed medical professional, and nothing here is medical advice.

The honest word in all of this is "yet."

Read the topics in any order. Each stands alone, and together they explain why longer, healthier lives are a realistic hope rather than a fantasy.
