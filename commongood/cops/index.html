Volunteer information

Rooted in Easton. Open to every reader.

Easton’s Reading Room opened in June 2011. Today, its work brings children, families and community readers together.

A good book. A place to belong.

Cops 'n' Kids Reading Room Easton connects children and their community through literacy. Families can share stories, take part in reading activities, and choose free books to enjoy at home.

Visit the Reading Room at Easton Area Community Center or look for story activities in the Discovery Room at Sigal Museum. Reading Room visits are free. Please confirm the current schedule before making a trip.

Books bring neighbors together

Readers, book donors, volunteers, educators, and local police officers all have a part to play. Give a new or gently used children's book, help prepare books for distribution, or share your time as a reader.

Reading with the people around us

The Reading Room connects access to books with relationships. Its work brings children together with family members, educators, volunteers and police officers through shared literacy activities. Learn how the program developed, plan a visit or find practical ways to contribute.
