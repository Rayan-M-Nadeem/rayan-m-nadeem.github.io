Reading together

The Reading Room gives families a place to explore books and share stories. Literacy activities and community readers help make that experience part of everyday life in Easton.

Books beyond the Reading Room

Book donations are sorted, labeled, and stored for distribution. The organization supplies books to children through local schools, organization libraries, reading programs, and community events.

Learning with community members

Police officers, high school and college students, retired teachers, and other volunteers take part in literacy activities. Community collaboration brings readers into schools and connects families with people who share their interest in children's learning.

For program themes and special activities, use the official news and social-media updates. Contact the Reading Room before planning a group visit.

Reading together at home

The organization's parent resources encourage adults to make reading a shared activity: spend time with a book, talk about what is happening and give children opportunities to choose stories. Its resource page provides the original guidance and educational links for families.

Schools, groups and community readers

Educators and group leaders can discuss a visit or a literacy activity connected with a classroom topic. Contact the Reading Room to arrange the date, location and activities. Older downloadable visit forms are currently unavailable; email or call rather than rely on a broken download.
