Connecting kids and community through literacy

Cops 'n' Kids of Easton helps children build a connection with books and with the people around them. Its work centers on access to books at home, positive relationships between children and police, and creative experiences that make reading a shared activity.

The international Cops 'n' Kids program began with Racine, Wisconsin, police officer Julia Burney Witherspoon in 1997. Easton's Reading Room opened on June 4, 2011, with financial sponsorship from Easton Area Police Athletic League and support from Easton Area Community Center, the Weed and Seed Program, and other community resources.

A community effort

Judith A. Dickerson is listed as director by the organization and in recent partner information. Volunteers include readers, educators, students, and neighbors who help collect, prepare, and distribute books. The Reading Room's collaboration with Easton Area Community Center remains central to its work.

Judith Dickerson's commitment to reading

Director Judith A. Dickerson's published message describes a career supporting children and young adults through speech therapy, education, nonprofit work and college administration. After retirement, she became involved with the literacy initiative in 2009.

Her message connects reading with independence and critical thinking. The Reading Room's idea of Freadom is about access to books and the freedom to imagine possibilities. Easton's 2011 opening brought together the Police Athletic League, EACC, Weed and Seed and community volunteers.
