From the archive: stories and community

In November 2025, the organization reported a Día de los Muertos story hour and craft activity in Sigal Museum's Discovery Room. Families explored a cultural tradition through books, conversation, and a related craft.

The organization also announced a free Story Walk on July 19, 2025, beginning at Sigal Museum's Discovery Room. The activity followed Easton's KidsPath art trail, combining reading, walking, and local wall art. These are past activities, not current registrations.
