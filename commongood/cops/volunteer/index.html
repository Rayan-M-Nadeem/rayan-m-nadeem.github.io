Share your time and your love of books

Volunteers help collect and deliver books, sort and label donations, staff the Reading Room, read with children, support family events, and coordinate book drives. There are practical ways to contribute both behind the scenes and alongside families.

Apply through Cops 'n' Kids

Use the organization's existing online volunteer application or download its PDF form. The official page says completed paper applications may be scanned and emailed or brought to the Reading Room during open hours.

The organization requires a Pennsylvania Child Abuse Certificate. Contact Cops 'n' Kids about the current clearance and onboarding requirements before beginning a role. Applications and screening remain with the organization; this proposal does not collect applicant information.

Work that keeps books circulating

Behind a reading activity are book collections, labeling, sorting, deliveries and preparation. Readers and event volunteers also work alongside children and families. The organization publishes volunteer accounts showing how that work has developed over time.

Read those volunteer stories. Use the current application and ask about clearances before beginning a role.
