Plan a visit

The Reading Room is on the lower level of Easton Area Community Center, 901 Washington Street, Easton, PA 18042. Families are welcome to read together and choose free books to take home.

The latest dated EACC schedule on the organization's website lists Thursdays, 2:00–5:00 p.m. Other published pages show different hours, so please confirm before visiting: call (484) 272-5090 or email copsnkidseaston@gmail.com.

Another place to share a story

Cops 'n' Kids also offers reading activities in the Discovery Room at Sigal Museum, 342 Northampton Street, Easton, PA 18042. Check the organization's current program updates for that location's schedule and story-hour details.

Visits for schools and groups

Teachers and group leaders can ask about planned storytelling, guest authors, or literacy activities connected with a classroom topic. Contact the organization to arrange a visit and discuss books for your group.

During a visit

Families can explore children's books, read together and choose books to take home. Published accounts differ about the number available per visit, so ask the Reading Room about today's arrangements rather than rely on an old allowance.

The EACC Reading Room and Sigal Museum's Discovery Room have separate published schedules. Confirm both the time and location before traveling, especially for a school or group visit.
