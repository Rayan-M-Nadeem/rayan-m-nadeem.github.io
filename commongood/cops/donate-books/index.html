Give a book its next reader

Cops 'n' Kids accepts new and gently used children's books. Books your family has outgrown, an extra book purchased while shopping, or a collection gathered through a book drive can help children build home libraries.

Before you drop off

The official book-donation page lists Easton Area Community Center at 901 Washington Street; Easton Police Department at 48 North Fourth Street; and Family YMCA of Easton/Phillipsburg and Vicinity at 1225 West Lafayette Street as collection locations. Confirm current acceptance and drop-off arrangements with Cops 'n' Kids before transporting a large donation.

Hold a book drive

Schools, workplaces, and community groups can collect books together. Use the organization's book-drive guidance and contact the Reading Room to coordinate delivery.

Financial contributions

Financial gifts help purchase new books for special programs and program-partner requests. Use the official contributions page for the organization's PayPal donation option and check instructions. Checks are made payable to Cops 'n' Kids of Easton. Do not send money to CommonGood.
