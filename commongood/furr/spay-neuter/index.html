FURR Fix: low-cost care in Bangor.

FURR Fix provides low-cost spay and neuter services for pet and community cats at 9 N Main Street, Bangor, PA 18013. It is a cats-only clinic, and appointments are arranged through FURR’s official clinic information.

Request an appointment through FURR

The official clinic page explains appointment requests, eligibility, current package prices, payment methods, and what to bring. Read that page before submitting a request. Availability and waiting times can change.

Help for community cats

FURR provides Trap-Neuter-Return information and clinic appointments for community cats. The rescue says it can guide and support trapping efforts but usually cannot provide on-site trapping services.

More care resources

FURR maintains a directory of other low-cost veterinary services and related resources. Contact the provider directly to confirm services, eligibility, and current pricing.

FURR FiX: use the right appointment pathway

The cats-only clinic is at 9 North Main Street, Bangor, Pennsylvania. Official information separates pet-cat and community-cat appointments and maintains the current services, prices, requirements and day-of instructions. Follow those instructions directly rather than relying on a copied checklist.
