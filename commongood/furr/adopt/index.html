Browse the official listings

Explore all cats, kittens, adult cats, seniors, cats with special needs, and cats seeking a home together. These links open FURR’s own listings; a photographed cat in this proposal is not a statement of current availability.

The next step is an application

Complete the adoption application on FURR’s official website. The application helps volunteers understand your household and the kind of companion you are looking for. The official application may ask you to create or sign in to a FURR website account.

Meet with FURR

Adoption events and arranged meetings offer a chance to meet cats. Contact FURR to confirm where a particular cat will be and discuss the process before visiting.
