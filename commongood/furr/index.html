Care happens through people.

Foster homes and adoption habitats support FURR’s rescue work without a public shelter building.

Meet your next companion

Start with FURR’s official, current cat listings. Browse cats and kittens, read their individual profiles, and continue to the organization’s own adoption application. Availability is maintained by FURR rather than duplicated here.

Room to recover. Room to belong.

Fostering makes rescue possible without a shelter building. Volunteers also care for adoption habitats, provide transportation, help with fundraising, and support the FURR Fix spay and neuter clinic.

Care beyond adoption

FURR Fix in Bangor offers low-cost spay and neuter appointments for pet and community cats. FURR also shares information about Trap-Neuter-Return and practical resources for cat caregivers.
