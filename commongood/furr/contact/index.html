Talk with FURR.

For questions about adoption, fostering, volunteering, donations, or rescue resources, contact Feline Urban Rescue and Rehab directly. This independent proposal does not receive applications or arrange placements.

Email and telephone

Email info@felineurbanrescueandrehab.org or call (610) 881-6700. Use the official application or intake links when a request needs a form.

Mailing address

Feline Urban Rescue and Rehab, Inc., P.O. Box 144, Stewartsville, NJ 08886. This is a mailing address, not a public shelter or cat drop-off location.

Clinic and adoption visits

FURR Fix is at 9 N Main Street, Bangor, PA 18013. Use the clinic page to request an appointment. Adoption meetings take place through fosters, adoption habitats, and events; confirm arrangements with FURR.

Follow the official organization

FURR’s website links to its Friends of Feline Urban Rescue & Rehab Facebook page. Use official channels for the most recent announcements.
