A clear path from first look to adoption.

Choosing a companion begins with a conversation about your home, your current pets, and what kind of cat will fit your life. FURR’s application and volunteer team guide the process.

Visitor information

Cat availability is maintained on FURR’s official site. Applications may require an account with FURR. This proposal does not collect applications or guarantee a placement.

Read the details

1. Look through current profiles

Browse FURR’s official cat listings and read the individual descriptions. Contact the rescue about a particular cat or the kind of match you hope to find.

2. Apply through FURR

Use the official online application. If you provide a veterinary reference, FURR asks that you call your veterinarian and give permission for the office to speak with the rescue. Personal application information stays in FURR’s existing system.

3. Arrange a meeting

Cats may attend different adoption events or be available for an arranged meeting with their foster family. Confirm arrangements with FURR instead of assuming every cat will be at every event.

Care and adoption fees

FURR’s adoption information describes the veterinary care included with adoption, including spay or neuter, microchipping, testing, and age-appropriate vaccinations. Check the official page for the current fee schedule and full terms before applying.
