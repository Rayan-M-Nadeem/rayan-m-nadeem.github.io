Home / Foster

Make room for a cat who needs time.

FURR does not have a shelter building. Foster families provide temporary homes for kittens and adult cats while volunteers arrange care and work toward an appropriate placement.

Different cats need different homes

Foster opportunities include kittens, mothers with kittens, adult cats between homes, and cats who need patience as they become comfortable with people. FURR can discuss which commitment fits your experience and household.

Support and responsibilities

FURR pays veterinary expenses at its approved vets and has a volunteer network to help new fosters. Foster caregivers need to take an active role in transporting cats to veterinary appointments. Review the official foster information for the complete expectations.

Found a cat you can foster?

The Finder Foster program is intended for people who have found a stray cat or kittens and can offer a temporary home. It has its own form and responsibilities. Completing an inquiry does not guarantee that FURR can accept a cat.

Long-term companionship for seniors

Seniors 4 Seniors is a long-term foster program for eligible senior residents in Northampton County, Pennsylvania, and Warren County, New Jersey. FURR arranges medical care while the caregiver provides daily care. Read the official eligibility and transportation requirements.

Finder Foster is a different commitment

The Finder Foster Program is for someone already caring for a cat or kittens they found. It is separate from receiving a foster animal from FURR. The published program describes approved veterinary care through FURR, with the finder providing food, litter, transport, photographs and participation in adoption events.

Read the official Finder Foster details

Seniors 4 Seniors

FURR also describes long-term fostering of a senior cat for a qualifying senior citizen on a fixed income, with medical care maintained by the rescue. Service-area and transport requirements apply, and FURR determines suitability through its own process.
