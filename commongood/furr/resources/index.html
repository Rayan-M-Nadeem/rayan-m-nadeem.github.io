Find the right next step for your cat.

FURR’s resources cover adoption, community cats, lost pets, veterinary care, and requests for help. These links preserve the organization’s existing information and intake pathways.

Living with a cat

Find FURR’s resources for introducing a new cat, information about FIV, and links to veterinary guidance. Use a veterinarian for advice about an individual animal’s health.

Lost or found

FURR provides lost-pet resources and a separate process for people who have found a stray cat. If you can care for a found cat temporarily, review the Finder Foster program as well.

Request help with rehoming

Returning a cat adopted from FURR and requesting help with a non-FURR cat use different official pathways. Contact the rescue and follow the appropriate instructions; submitting an inquiry does not confirm intake or a placement.

Barn Cat program

FURR’s Barn Cat program seeks appropriate farm or homestead placements for cats who need that setting. Property, shelter, daily care, and acclimation requirements apply. Read the official details and apply through FURR.
