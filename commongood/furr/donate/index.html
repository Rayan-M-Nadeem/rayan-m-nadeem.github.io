Home / Donate

Support the care behind every placement.

Donations help FURR pay veterinary bills and provide food, litter, cleaning supplies, and support for community-cat care. Use FURR’s own donation page and approved channels to contribute.

AdoptFosterClinic & TNRNeed help?Volunteer

Give through the official donation page

FURR publishes its online donation options on its existing website. Start there for the current approved methods. A verified Venmo destination is also linked from that page. This proposal does not process payments.

Send a contribution by mail

The official mailing address is Feline Urban Rescue and Rehab, Inc., P.O. Box 144, Stewartsville, NJ 08886. FURR also accepts donations at its adoption events.

Send something useful

FURR’s wish-list page links to an Amazon wish list and a separate Kitten Shower wish list. If you have supplies to donate, contact FURR to arrange delivery or ask about collection. Confirm the dated event schedule before bringing items to an event.

Other ways to support care

FURR's official site offers sponsorship for cats with medical needs or other barriers to placement. Its shopping page groups published wish lists and shopping-support programs. Check those official pages for current needs and recipient information.
