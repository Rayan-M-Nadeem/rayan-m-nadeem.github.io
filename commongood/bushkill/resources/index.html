Guides for neighbors and landowners

Download the watershed overview, streamside-buffer guides, water-quality brochure and Two Rivers birding map. These are established educational publications; check each document’s date before applying technical or regulatory guidance.

Research and conservation plans

The reading library includes the 2009 Upper Bushkill Coldwater Conservation Plan, the 2005 Sobers Run plan, the 2013 riparian-buffer report and the Two Rivers Area Greenways Plan. Keep their original dates visible: these records explain the history and scientific context of conservation decisions.

A useful document library

The official library preserves watershed and streamside-buffer brochures, birding and natural-history maps, coldwater conservation plans and restoration reports. Keep a publication's year attached to it: a conservation plan is a historical planning resource, not a live measurement.

Look for the 2005 Sobers Run plan, the 2009 Upper Bushkill plan, the 2013 riparian-buffer report and the Two Rivers plan and overview map in the official resource library.

Open the Conservancy's brochures

Plants, birds and stream health

The natural-history resources describe local habitats, native flora, invasive species and aquatic insects. The published birding account and wildflower list are reference material. Their historical descriptions should not be converted into current species counters.
