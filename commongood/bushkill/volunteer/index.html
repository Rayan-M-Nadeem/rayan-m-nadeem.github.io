Make room for the creek in your month

The Conservancy is organized and staffed by volunteers. You can contribute through field work, community education, research or the practical work that keeps a local organization running.

The watershedProject archiveMonitoringDocument libraryGet involved

Ways to take part

Monthly water testing usually takes less than two hours, with training and supplies provided. Other roles include educational outreach, planting and invasive-plant removal, tree-tube maintenance, grant writing and board participation. Use the official interest form to discuss a role and current needs.

Meet the Conservancy

BSC typically meets at 7 p.m. on the fourth Monday of the month, upstairs at Tatamy Municipal Building. The organization asks visitors to confirm meeting details through its official Facebook page or by contacting BSC.

More than a cleanup

The Conservancy's official volunteer form includes streamside maintenance, municipal advocacy, outreach, rain-garden care, litter cleanup, water testing, grant writing, historical documentation, environmental research and board service.

The published monitoring information says training and supplies are provided. Ask about current sites, training and the time commitment before joining.
