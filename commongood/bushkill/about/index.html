Neighbors working for the Bushkill

The Conservancy is an all-volunteer public-private partnership. Its work brings together people and organizations with a shared interest in the future of Bushkill Creek.

The watershedProject archiveMonitoringDocument libraryGet involved

From a trail idea to a watershed partnership

The organization began with a committee exploring a recreational trail along abandoned rail lines beside the lower creek. That work revealed broader needs, including protecting the greenway. Incorporated in 1991, the Conservancy grew into a nonprofit focused on the whole watershed.

A shared effort

Rather than operating through a conventional membership model, BSC developed a partnership approach involving educational institutions, nonprofit groups, municipalities, government agencies and individual Friends. Its historical records are largely held in print; contact the organization to ask about research access or sharing creek-related documents.
