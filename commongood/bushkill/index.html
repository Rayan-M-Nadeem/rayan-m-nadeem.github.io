Northampton County, Pennsylvania · Since 1991

A creek worth caring for.

From wooded headwaters to the Delaware River in Easton, Bushkill Creek connects the places we call home. The Bushkill Stream Conservancy brings volunteers and community partners together to care for its watershed.
