Home / The watershed

One watershed. Many connections.

The Bushkill Creek watershed covers about 80 square miles in Northampton County, Pennsylvania, reaching from Blue Mountain to the Delaware River in Easton. This is the Northampton County Bushkill—not the creek of the same name in Monroe County.

The watershedProject archiveMonitoringDocument libraryGet involved

From headwaters to Easton

Wooded slopes and groundwater recharge help supply the headwaters in Bushkill, Plainfield and Moore townships. Carbonate springs contribute cool summer flows. Schoeneck Creek drains the western watershed near Nazareth, while the creek passes through agricultural, developed and urban landscapes on its way south.

Explore with reliable local maps

Use the Conservancy’s published watershed map to understand the creek and its tributaries. The official visit guide introduces public trails and access areas, including Jacobsburg Environmental Education Center, Plainfield Township Bike Path, Palmer trails and Easton’s Karl Stirner Arts Trail. Check the relevant land manager for current access conditions.

Explore the creek responsibly

The official visit guide covers Jacobsburg, Plainfield Township Bike Path, Palmer Rails-to-Trails, the Karl Stirner Arts Trail, Blue Mountain and local parks. Confirm current access and rules with the land manager before a trip.

The Conservancy's natural-history pages discuss macroinvertebrates, birds, native plants and invasive species. These are useful background resources, not live surveys or current water-safety advice.
