Restoration, rooted in place

The Conservancy’s documented projects connect stream habitat, stormwater management, public access and native planting. The examples below are a project history, not an assertion that every project remains underway.

The watershedProject archiveMonitoringDocument libraryGet involved

Sullivan Park stormwater wetland

BSC worked with the City of Easton, Lafayette College and Northampton County Conservation District on engineering and construction of a detention wetland. The work addressed stormwater damage while creating habitat and retaining recreational uses. Sullivan Park received Pennsylvania’s 2014 Green Park award.

Native planting along the creek

At Palmer Park at the Villages of Mill Race, the Conservancy documents invasive-plant removal, native shrubs and trees, and warm-season meadows. At the Bushkill–Delaware confluence, native bank planting and an educational sign connected stream restoration with public understanding.

Creek restoration with local partners

A 2021 Wildlands Conservancy report documents restoration and habitat work with BSC and Forks of the Delaware Trout Unlimited between Tatamy and Palmer Township. Streambank stabilization, expanded buffers and habitat structures were part of that effort. Read the dated project record for its scope and context.

Protecting Henry’s Woods

A documented Growing Greener project at Jacobsburg addressed eroding banks with stabilization measures, trail barriers and native restoration planting. Keep this project history separate from any newly announced work at the park.

Plans, planting and streambank care

The official project archive documents more than the four projects described above. Coldwater plans, the Two Rivers Area Greenways work, open-space planning and streambank improvements show different parts of the Conservancy's history.

In 2012, a Coldwater Heritage Partnership grant and local support helped plant more than 1,500 willow and dogwood live stakes along Bushkill Creek and Sobers Run. Other accounts cover native planting near the Delaware confluence, invasive removal in Palmer Township and bank protection at Henry's Woods. These are documented past projects, not invitations to a newly scheduled workday.
