When volunteers help

The organization welcomes volunteers age 16 and older. Help is needed with setup the day before the celebration and during both holiday seatings.

Register your interest

Open the 2026 volunteer sign-up form to see the available arrangements and provide your details. Specific tasks and timing are coordinated by the Project Hope team.

Have a question first?

Contact the team if you are interested in volunteering as a group or want to ask about the day. Please use the official volunteer form for registration.

Ask where help is needed this season

Preparing and hosting the holiday gathering involves more than serving the meal. Gift preparation, winter essentials, setup and the guest experience are part of the organization's published account of its work. Contact Project Hope or use its approved volunteer pathway for current assignments, age requirements and arrival instructions.
