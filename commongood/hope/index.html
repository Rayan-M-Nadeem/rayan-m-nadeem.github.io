Rooted right here

An Easton tradition, made by neighbors.

Project Hope began in 1989 with local people preparing a meal and inviting others to share it. That simple idea remains at the heart of the organization: making room for neighbors during the holiday season.

Today, volunteers, families, businesses, and community groups help carry the tradition forward in Easton and the Lehigh Valley.
