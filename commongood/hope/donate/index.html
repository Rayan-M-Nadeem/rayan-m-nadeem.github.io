Gifts and winter essentials

The holiday celebration includes children’s gifts, coats, blankets, and other winter items. Read the wish list and contact the team before bringing items so they can confirm current needs and arrangements.

Food and other contributions

Monetary donations help purchase groceries for the meal. If you would like to offer food, a business contribution, or another in-kind donation, ask the team what can be accepted and how to arrange it.

Donation wish list

Winter essentials

Project Hope’s clothing collection has included:

  • New and gently used coats
  • New socks
  • Handmade hats, gloves, and scarves
  • Blankets

Children’s gifts

The celebration includes gifts for children from newborns through teenagers. Contact the team to ask which gifts are needed this season and how to arrange a contribution.

Before you bring a donation

Please confirm current needs, item conditions, and delivery arrangements with Project Hope. The PO Box is for mail, not a donation drop-off location.
