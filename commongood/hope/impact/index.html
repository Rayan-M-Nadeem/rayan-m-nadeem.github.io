A tradition carried forward

Since its beginnings in 1989, Project Hope has continued through local volunteers and community support. Its holiday gathering combines a meal with gifts, winter essentials, and entertainment.

Responding in 2020

During the pandemic, Project Hope also helped families through meal vouchers and support from local restaurants. Lehigh Valley Press documented that response in May 2020. This is part of the organization’s history, rather than a current holiday registration program.
