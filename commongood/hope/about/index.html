A community tradition since 1989.

Project Hope is a volunteer-run nonprofit rooted in Easton, Pennsylvania, and the neighbors who make its holiday celebration possible.

How it began

In 1989, a local family and community members cooked a holiday meal and opened their doors to anyone who needed one. The gathering grew through the work of returning volunteers and supporters.

Judy Walker later helped longstanding volunteers continue the event, organize a board, establish the nonprofit, and bring in corporate support. The organization remains entirely volunteer-run.

What brings us together

Project Hope welcomes people in need from Easton and the Lehigh Valley. Its holiday work includes a meal, gifts for children, winter clothing, and entertainment. Adults are part of the welcome, too.

Local businesses, families, schools, community groups, and individual donors all have a place in that work. The sponsor acknowledgments recognize the established relationships behind the celebration.

People behind Project Hope

The organization’s published board lists Judy Walker (President), Barbara Dozier, Susan Brooks, Eileen Taylor-Ganssle, Andrea White, Alesha Pretopapa, Stan Walker, Lonnie Smith, Jenny Calvert, and John A. Calvert Jr.

Many kinds of volunteer work

The organization's published biographies show the work behind the celebration: collecting children's gifts and clothing, preparing the winter boutique, knitting items and arranging hospitality. These are personal contributions over time, rather than a list of roles currently open for registration.

Read the volunteers' own accounts on Project Hope's official About page.
