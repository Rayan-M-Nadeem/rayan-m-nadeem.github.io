Register for Parents’ Date Night

Please contact the organization for current registration details and availability.

Call 610-253-8271

Use the application process confirmed by staff. This proposal does not collect applications.

The original notice describes a three-hour Friday program intended to become monthly, depending on demand. The first event was a dance party with a DJ, lighting and smoke effects, and a 360 photo booth. Proposed later activities included dodgeball, Nerf games, and themed events.

For children already enrolled in after-school or sports programs, the notice lists $20 per child and a $40 family maximum. River Grille offered a free-appetizer coupon to participating parents and family members, provided at drop-off. Payment options included listed payment apps or in-person payment.

Use the original form below and confirm the date, offer, and availability with EACC before booking.
