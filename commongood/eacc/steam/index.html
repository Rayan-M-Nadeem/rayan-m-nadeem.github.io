More about this program

STEAM connects science, technology, engineering, the arts, and mathematics through practical projects. EACC describes an approach built around questions, creative work, collaboration, and solving problems.

The project archive includes earth sciences, robots, simple machines and transportation, drama and dance, music, coding, and math activities. Several examples are dated, including material from 2019, so the archive is a record of the center’s learning experiences rather than a timetable for this week.

Explore the STEAM page to see that history and ask the center which projects are part of the current Clubhouse or camp program.
