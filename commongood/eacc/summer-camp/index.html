Latest notice

The existing page says the eight-week 2026 camp has ended and invites families back in 2027. It lists $400 per child, breakfast and lunch, field trips, discounts for returning campers and siblings, and an additional charge for arrival before 9 a.m. Confirm the next year’s dates and fees before registering.

Camp history

The 2024 account reports more than 80 children, with dance, filmmaking, yoga, sports, science, swimming, and Summer Olympics. Earlier accounts describe gardening, reading, coding, Da Vinci STEAM, Cooking with Kellyn, and a kickball championship.

Trips included the zoo, mines, caverns, planetarium, museums, Da Vinci Science Center, Herr’s factory, Nurture Nature Center, Easton Public Market, and Heil Pool. Cops ’n’ Kids offered Passport to the World reading activities.

Historical listings describe ages 5–14, 131 campers in 2019 and 78 in 2022, and Monday–Friday hours of 8 a.m.–4 p.m. A separate 2025 notice gives 9 a.m.–4 p.m.; verify next-season hours. Photos from these summers are preserved below.

More about this program

Summer camp brings educational and recreational activities together across the school break. The center’s account of summer 2026 describes an eight-week program with activities and field trips. Its earlier camp reports include art, dance, filmmaking, science, sports, reading, gardening, pool visits, and educational outings.

Past destinations have included science centers, museums, a planetarium, and the zoo. These examples show the range of camp experiences; they are not a guaranteed itinerary for the next summer.

The camp page now invites families to ask about 2027 and lists meals, fees, early arrival, and possible discounts. Use that page and contact the center for the next camp’s confirmed dates, places, ages, and arrangements.
