More about this program

Who it’s for: Children in kindergarten through eighth grade.

What to expect: Saints Clubhouse brings together homework help, art, music, reading, and physical activity in the gym. Families can also choose books or read together in the downstairs Cops ’n’ Kids Reading Room. Summer camp offers another way to take part beyond the school year.

Getting started: Visit the after-school page for the school-year program, or the summer-camp page for seasonal information. Contact the center to ask about spaces, program times, and registration.

A place between school and home The Saints Clubhouse combines educational and recreational activities for K–8 students. EACC describes its role as supporting children academically, socially, emotionally, and physically, while giving working parents and caregivers a place where school assignments and supervised activities continue after the classroom day.

Projects as well as homework The Clubhouse’s learning approach draws on positive youth development, leadership, academic standards, and developmental assets. Its STEAM work connects science, technology, engineering, the arts, and mathematics. The center’s project archive includes robots, simple machines and transportation, earth sciences, coding and math activities, and performances involving drama, dance, and music. These are examples of its program history; the project mix changes over time.

Mentoring and shared learning Staff lead the program, with college and community volunteers contributing additional support. The Clubhouse describes longstanding relationships with Lafayette and Penn State volunteers, alongside partnerships with organizations including the school district, 4-H, and the YMCA.

Reading belongs here, too The lower-level Cops ’n’ Kids Reading Room gives families another way to spend time with books. Homework, creative projects, physical activity, and reading are connected parts of the center’s educational work.
