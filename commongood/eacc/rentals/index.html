Building and sports floors

The existing rate is $50 per hour for a floor, with discounts for full-building bookings, longer rentals, and members. Spaces can accommodate basketball, wrestling, dodgeball, soccer, pickleball, gymnastics, tumbling, and cheerleading. Parties can combine upper-floor games with first-floor activities and a 360 photo booth.

Transportation

The center lists a 2012 Freightliner bus for up to 48 passengers, at $100 per hour with a two-hour minimum. Trips depend on driver and bus availability and can include the zoo, wineries, or longer field trips.

360 photo booth

Off-site rental is listed at $50 per hour with a two-hour minimum.

Confirm availability, rates, and booking requirements with EACC staff or awilliams@eaccpa.org before making plans.
