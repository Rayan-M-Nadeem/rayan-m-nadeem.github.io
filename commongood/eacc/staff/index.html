Maria Laboy and Mayche Webster

Senior Program Coordinators. They lead activities, programs, and field trips for children, older adults, and families, with an emphasis on daily support and participation.

Food Director. Derek prepares healthy meals, works with children, and supports mentoring at EACC.

Judy Dickerson

Cops ’n’ Kids Director. Judy leads the reading center and children’s literacy work. The previous site notes her first annual George Wolf Public Education Award, presented by Paxinosa principal Mr. Viglianti, and recognition for contributions to public education.
