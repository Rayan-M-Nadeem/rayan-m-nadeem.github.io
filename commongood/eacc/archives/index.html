The previous archive includes home activities for children and adults, with movement and dance videos from GoNoodle, PBS programming through Lehigh Valley Learns, and online storytimes.

Reading and entertainment resources include Harry the Dirty Dog, Catching the Moon, Pixar performers reading Toy Story books, Disney storytime, It’s a Small World, drawing Spider-Man, At Home with Olaf, and the JAMMitors. The original resource links remain listed below.

The archive also records the ShopRite food donation coordinated by Joe Colalillo, Dean Young, and Jake Williams, and a Christmas toy-drive donation from The Shawn Harvey Morning Show.
