Crayola Garden: historical account

The previous page links a 2012 story about Crayola’s garden. It reports 1,200 pounds of produce donated in the preceding year and an expected 2,400-pound harvest. The account also describes EACC serving 3,000 meals monthly during the school year and 2,000 monthly in summer. These figures describe that earlier period.

Garden photos and the original article links are retained below.

More about this program

The children’s community garden connects summer-camp activities with growing food and caring for plants. EACC describes campers learning with Master Gardeners from Penn State Cooperative Extension, starting seeds in a greenhouse and following them into flower gardens or vegetable harvests.

The garden gives the center’s science and food activities a practical connection: children can see how something grown from a seed becomes a plant or an ingredient. The program page preserves photographs and the history of its gardening work; ask the center about the next opportunities to participate.
