Privacy

The previous policy states that personal and mobile information is used for EACC purposes and is not shared with third parties for marketing. It describes HTTPS connections and gives 610-253-8271 for privacy questions.

Text messages

The existing terms describe autodialed program and center updates, variable message frequency, and possible message or data charges. They say agreement is not a purchase condition and relates to participation while a child is registered. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for information.

Civil rights

The original page links the USDA nondiscrimination statement covering race, color, national origin, sex, disability, age, and retaliation for earlier civil-rights activity. The complete official statement and the center’s original policy wording remain linked below.

This page summarizes the existing notices; review their complete wording at the linked sources.
