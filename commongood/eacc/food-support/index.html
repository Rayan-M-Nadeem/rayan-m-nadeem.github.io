The previous homepage lists food distribution every other Friday at noon, using January 23 as the start of the alternating schedule. Contact EACC to confirm the next distribution date before visiting.

It thanks Giant Food Stores for support, and Maria, Mayche, and Chef Derrick for their leadership. Earlier news also records a ShopRite donation from Joe Colalillo, coordinated with Dean Young of the Boys & Girls Club and collected by Jake Williams.

Meals are also part of several EACC programs. See the after-school, summer camp, and Silver Connections pages for their specific information.

More about this program

Food distribution is another part of the center’s practical support for the neighborhood. EACC’s announcement thanks Giant Food Stores and describes an alternating-Friday distribution at noon. Confirm the next date directly with the center rather than calculating a visit from an older announcement.

Meals also connect several of EACC’s programs: the summer-camp page includes breakfast and lunch, and Silver Connections describes a morning snack and prepared lunch. The food-support page brings the distribution information together in one place.
