Comedy Night

The center’s fundraiser at The Vault Brewing Co. has its own page with the original ticket links. Fundraising opportunities also include winery outings and festival booths serving Dirty Soda. Contact Adam Williams at awilliams@eaccpa.org to participate.

Earlier notices

The old events page lists Summer Camp 2025 from June 16–August 9, 9 a.m.–4 p.m., and a Casino Night Gala with a tentative first-Friday-in-September 2025 date. These dates have passed.

It also lists fundraising bingo at Palmer Firehouse on Tuesdays at 7 p.m. Another older news page lists Jaybird Bingo in Bethlehem on Fridays and Sundays. Confirm the current venue and schedule with EACC.
