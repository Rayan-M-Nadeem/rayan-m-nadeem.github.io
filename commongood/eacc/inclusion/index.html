About EACC

About EACCBoard & advisory boardDiversity, equity & inclusionCommunity partnersA message from the presidentMeet the staffOur supportersAll pages

EACC describes an inclusive, anti-racist organization where people of all ages and backgrounds work together and are treated with dignity. It welcomes participation regardless of ability, age, culture, ethnicity, faith, gender, gender expression or identity, ideology, income, national origin, race, or sexual orientation.

The commitment extends to staff and members: valuing different experiences, encouraging people to share perspectives, creating a sense of belonging, and putting equity and inclusion into everyday work. Its stated goal is to address community needs and social inequities while supporting each person’s development and well-being.
