More about this program

A community center for different stages of life Explore school-year learning and summer activities for children, Saints sports, and programs for older adults. There are also places to read together, learn English, and find food support.

The center is at 901 Washington Street in Easton’s West Ward. Use the program links below to find the right activity, or call 610-253-8271 to talk through your options with the team.
