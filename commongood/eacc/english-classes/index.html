More about this program

The center hosts free English classes presented by M.I.R.A Resources. The class flyer gives EACC’s Washington Street address and a Saturday 10 a.m. meeting time; the homepage lists Saturday sessions through December 2026.

M.I.R.A’s wider mission is to connect migrant, immigrant, refugee, and asylee families with local resources and community support. Open the class page for the original flyer and the provider’s website, then confirm the next session and any enrollment arrangements with the organizers.
