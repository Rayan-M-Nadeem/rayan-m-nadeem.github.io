What happens at the center?

EACC is an independent nonprofit serving Easton’s West Ward. Its work starts with a safe, supportive place for young people, with adults who help them learn, build confidence, and find their interests.

Silver Connections

Older adults looking for activities, conversation, and time with other people in the neighborhood.

Silver Connections offers cards, bingo, crafts, music, fitness activities such as chair yoga, and educational talks.

A neighborhood story, still being written

Some Easton families still know this place as St. Anthony’s Youth Center. The name has changed as the center’s work has grown, but Washington Street remains its home.

Find your place at the center

Families can explore the Saints Clubhouse, school-year activities and summer programming. Saints athletics has its own sports pages. Silver Connections brings older adults together, while the Reading Room, English classes, food support and community garden connect neighbors in other ways.
