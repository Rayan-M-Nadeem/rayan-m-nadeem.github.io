Out-of-school-time program grant

EACC reports $182,206 in state funding through Senator Lisa Boscola, paid across two years, for after-school and summer camp programs. The center credits volunteer Kathy Presto and the senator’s team. The announcement is linked below.

Window renovation: 2019

An October 2, 2019 report by Rudy Miller describes replacement of 25 windows at 901 Washington Street. Mayor Sal Panto Jr. took part in the September 30 ceremony. Clear, energy-efficient windows replaced translucent ones so children could see outside.

The city funded half the project; GDEP, Lafayette College, and an anonymous donor supported the remainder. Ramos Construction installed the windows. Maurice S. Luker III described the project’s meaning for children, and Tony Corallo discussed completion. LED wrestling-room lights were another energy-saving improvement. The report dates the center’s opening to 1953.

The resulting Open a New Window campaign supported programs for children and older adults. Read the linked original coverage for the complete reporting.
