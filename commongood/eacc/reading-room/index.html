Listed opening hours

Thursdays: 11 a.m.–7 p.m. First and third Saturdays: 10 a.m.–noon.

Special activities are listed on the program’s monthly flyer. Call 610-250-6562 or check the partner’s website before visiting.

Literacy and community

The reading room opened to the public in June 2011. The previous page reports more than 80,000 books distributed. Its work expands children’s access to books at home, builds positive relationships with police, and offers creative literacy experiences for families.

Director Judith A. Dickerson describes reading as a route to imagination, self-understanding, and opportunity. The original website, monthly flyer, and Facebook page are linked below.

More about this program

Cops ’n’ Kids provides a place for children and families to share stories and choose books on EACC’s lower level. The partner’s website says children may select up to three free books to take home on a visit, helping reading continue beyond the room itself.

Police officers, students, volunteers, and community organizations take part in the literacy activities. Teachers and group leaders can ask about a planned visit, storytelling, author appearances, or an activity connected to a classroom topic.

The partner lists Thursday public hours at EACC and special Saturday sessions at the Sigal Museum. These are different venues, so check the partner’s current flyer and location before traveling.
