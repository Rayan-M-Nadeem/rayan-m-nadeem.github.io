Play and participate

Each sport has a separate page with season information and registration links. EACC also describes SPARK, an exercise and nutrition program supporting healthy habits for children and adolescents.

Coaches and volunteers

The 2023–2024 coaching notice sought mentors for grades 3–12 and 10–15 basketball teams. Volunteer applications, background checks, and child-abuse clearances were required. Applications were free to volunteers. Coaches, players, and parents were expected to attend preseason meetings and follow the code of conduct; negative behavior toward players, officials, coaches, or parents was prohibited.

Earlier program notes

The athletics overview lists former Saint Todd Heller in connection with wrestling, thanks Lehigh Valley Orthopedic Institute, and gives an earlier $60 wrestling fee. The separate 2023–2024 wrestling page lists $70, so confirm current charges directly. Cheerleading coach Lisa Brown supported basketball and other sporting events.

More about this program

Who it’s for: Young people interested in joining the Saints through basketball, wrestling, cheerleading, or soccer. Age groups and availability depend on the sport and season.

What to expect: Team practices and sporting activities are part of the center’s athletics program. The basketball information also describes expectations for players, parents, and coaches, including respectful behavior.

Getting started: Choose a sport to find its registration information. Ask the center about the current season, age group, practice schedule, and any participation costs before signing up.

Basketball: league play and a younger-player option The 2026–2027 basketball announcement describes a ten-game Community Basketball League season followed by playoffs, with scrimmages adding playing time. It lists Paxinosa, Cheston, and March elementary schools as game or practice sites. Children in second grade and below have a separate format outside the CBL, with games using the program’s own officials.

Cheerleading, wrestling, and soccer The cheerleading page describes weekday practices at EACC and participation around the fall and winter sports season. Wrestling has its own team page. Soccer has also offered an eight-week program with separate groups for ages 6–9 and 10–15; its posted February–March 2026 dates are a past session, rather than a current enrollment promise.

The adults behind each team The basketball program identifies roles beyond coaching: keeping scorebooks, operating the scoreboard, taking photos or video, and fundraising. Its broader athletics information calls for volunteer applications, background checks, child-abuse clearances, and respectful conduct toward players, families, officials, and other coaches.

Choose the sport before choosing a season Each sport has its own page because eligibility, fees, venues, and registration windows differ. Follow the team links for the relevant announcement and contact EACC about current rosters and the next available session.
