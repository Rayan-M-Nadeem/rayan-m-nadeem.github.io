Community support

EACC thanked David and Vanessa Madochick of Rivals Sports Bar and Restaurant for their donation. A food donation from ShopRite’s Joe Colalillo was coordinated with Dean Young of the Boys & Girls Club and collected by Jake Williams.

Building and music updates

The old homepage reports new energy-efficient windows and a Pennsylvania senior-center grant toward first-floor renovations. It thanks Marvin Windows, Ramos Construction, DelVal Properties, Lafayette College, GDEP, and the City of Easton. Big Easy Easton Jazz Band rehearsed at EACC, performed for children, donated instruments, and offered music instruction.

Earlier notices

Archived items include a COVID vaccination clinic; AARP Tax-Aide document scanning and virtual appointments (1-888-227-7669); a May 7, 2021 gala heading and a separate May 2023 Northampton Country Club notice; Jaybird Bingo at 4406 Easton Avenue, Bethlehem, with 5 p.m. doors and 7 p.m. games; and summer camp in 2020 and 2023.

Other stories cover the November 16, 2019 Minecraft play at Paxinosa Auditorium, Halloween 2020, and Crayola TIP’s arts-and-crafts visit during EACC’s 8:30 a.m.–5 p.m. remote-learning program. These are historical notices, not current event announcements.
