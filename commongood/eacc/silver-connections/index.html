The center’s homepage lists Silver Connections on Tuesdays, Wednesdays, and Thursdays. Activities include cards, bingo, arts and crafts, music, chair yoga and senior fitness, educational talks, and quarterly trips.

The program page also describes presentations by local historians, a take-home book library, holiday gatherings, a breakfast snack, and a healthy lunch prepared by Chef Derek.

Maria Laboy and Mayche Webster coordinate the senior program. Call EACC to confirm daily times, participation details, and upcoming activities.

More about this program

Who it’s for: Older adults looking for activities, conversation, and time with other people in the neighborhood.

What to expect: Silver Connections offers cards, bingo, crafts, music, fitness activities such as chair yoga, and educational talks. The program also describes a take-home library, holiday gatherings, a breakfast snack, and lunch at the center.

Getting started: Open the program page to learn more, then contact EACC to confirm meeting times, participation details, and upcoming activities or outings.

A regular social setting Silver Connections brings together casual games, creative activities, and educational opportunities. Cards and bingo sit alongside arts and crafts, presentations, and a library of books members can borrow to read at home. Holiday gatherings and special events provide additional occasions to get together.

Movement, music, and outings The center also lists music, senior fitness activities such as chair yoga, educational lectures, and periodic field trips. These activities give the program variety; check with the coordinators about the current calendar and which activities are planned for a particular day.

Meals are part of the visit The program page describes a breakfast snack on arrival and a prepared lunch before participants leave. EACC’s staff page identifies Maria Laboy and Mayche Webster as senior-program coordinators and Derek Glenn as food director.

Planning your first visit The homepage lists Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday programming. Contact the center for arrival and departure times, participation arrangements, meal information, and the next activity or outing.
