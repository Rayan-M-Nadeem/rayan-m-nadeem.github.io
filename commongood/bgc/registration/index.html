For parents & guardians

Start here to join the club.

  1. Talk with the club.

    Call 610-253-5846 or contact the team about current membership availability.

  2. Ask about your child’s program.

    Confirm the current age group, schedule, fees, and the application materials staff need.

  3. Follow the club’s approved process.

    Staff can explain enrollment and arrange a visit. Please send personal information only through the process they confirm.

Before you join

Call 610-703-8575 or 484-239-2075 to ask about a tour, current availability, membership fees, and the paperwork needed to join. The original homepage welcomes families of children in full-day kindergarten through high school to find out more about the club.

What to ask

  • Which activities are running for your child’s age group?
  • What are the current session times and pickup arrangements?
  • What forms and documents are required?
  • What are the current membership and program fees?

From the 2023–24 announcement

The original homepage advertised fall, winter, and spring membership at $15. It listed grades K–5 sessions from 3 pm–5:45 pm and grades 6–12 from 6 pm–8:45 pm. These details belong to that earlier announcement and need confirmation for a new season.
