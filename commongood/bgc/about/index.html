Our purpose

The Club brings together caring leadership and youth programs to help young people and their families build fulfilling futures. Its work emphasizes learning, creativity, physical activity, health, character and community leadership.

A place to belong

The Club’s published mission calls for programming that is affordable, accessible, enjoyable and shaped around young people. It welcomes youth across backgrounds and combines academic, cultural, athletic, health, arts and spiritual enrichment.

More of our story

Young people and families can explore learning, creativity, leadership, and active play with the club’s team.
