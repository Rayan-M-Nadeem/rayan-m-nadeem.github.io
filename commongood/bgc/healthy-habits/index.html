Practical life skills

The Club’s published program covers nutrition, cooking, hygiene, healthy relationships and conflict resolution. Prevention activities also address bullying, tobacco, alcohol, drugs and teen pregnancy.

Food and movement

The original page describes after-school meals, summer breakfast, lunch and snacks, plus daily opportunities for physical activity. Confirm today’s meal schedule and dietary arrangements with staff.

2020–21 results archive

The Club reported that 65% of participating youth ate fruit five or more days a week, 63% ate vegetables that often, 97% drank water that often and 95% exercised that often. These are historical, self-reported program results.

Growing healthy habits together

Kids Café and Healthy Habits connect nutritious food with cooking, nutrition and everyday life skills. The club has described after-school meals, kitchen activities and summer breakfast, lunch and snacks as part of its work.

Talk with staff about the current meal schedule, dietary needs and available sessions.
