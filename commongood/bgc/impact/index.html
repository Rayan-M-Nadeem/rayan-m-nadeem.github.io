What makes a Club experience

The Club describes its approach through five elements: a safe and positive setting, enjoyment, supportive relationships, meaningful opportunities and expectations, and recognition.

Turning participation into progress

Activities and focused programs give young people repeated opportunities to learn and practice skills. The Club encourages regular attendance and connects its programming with education, health, leadership and personal development.
