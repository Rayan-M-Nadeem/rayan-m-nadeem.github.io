How the program works

The original page describes assessment, personalized online instruction, progress monitoring and educator resources. Learners receive practice matched to their reading needs, with additional support where needed.

Archived results

The Club reported the following participant counts and shares reading at or above grade level:2014–15:93 and 72%;2015–16:149 and 66%;2016–17:126 and 63%;2017–18:117 and 78%;2018–19:109 and 87%;2019–20:70 and 90%;2020–21:90 and 75%.
