Practicing with purpose

The Club describes DreamBox as a K–8 math resource that adapts lessons to student progress. Educators can use reports to review participation and work on mathematical understanding.

Archived participation

The Club’s original records list 86 students in 2017–18, 118 in 2018–19 and 88 in 2019–20. Reported average weekly participation was 66, 101 and 70 minutes, respectively.

These records describe earlier school-year and summer cohorts. Ask the Club about present-day access, age groups and scheduling.
