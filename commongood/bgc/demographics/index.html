Who the Club served: 2020

An archival snapshot of the young people represented in the Club’s published 2020 report.

Information to keep in mind

2020 archive. The published age categories sum to 418 rather than 463, and ethnicity percentages sum to 98.70%; source discrepancies require clarification, not invented corrections.

Reported participation

The original report lists 463 youth: 282 male and 181 female. Its age categories list 48 ages 5–8, 159 ages 9–12, 144 ages 13–16 and 67 ages 17–20.

Reported backgrounds

The report lists African American 49.89%; Hispanic/Latino 17.49%; two or more races 14.47%; White 10.80%; some other race 4.32%; Asian 0.86%; American Indian/Alaskan Native 0.65%; and Native Hawaiian/Pacific Islander 0.22%.

These figures describe the original reporting period and do not represent today’s enrollment.
