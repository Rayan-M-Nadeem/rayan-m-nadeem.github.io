Learning and future plans

Future Stars supports study habits and confidence. Power Hour, Project Learn, Lexia, Istation and DreamBox combine homework help and reading or math practice. Club TECH introduces computing, coding and everyday digital tools.

Leadership and relationships

BE Great Graduate, mentoring, Torch and Keystone Clubs offer career exploration, service and leadership. SMART Moves and Positive Action focus on choices, relationships and handling pressure.

Creative and active afternoons

Artworks includes visual arts, crafts, design, drama and music. Triple Play covers sport, fitness and recreation. Kids Café and Healthy Habits connect food, cooking, nutrition and life skills. Summer Brain Gain adds learning activities during the school break.

Ask the Club which activities are available for your child’s age and the current season.
