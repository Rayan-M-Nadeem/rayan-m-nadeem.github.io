Dance and performance

The Club describes dance sessions combining movement, rhythm and choreography. Young people can express ideas, work together and build confidence through performance.

Design and imagination

Fashion activities introduce clothing design, accessories and other creative projects. The program gives members space to explore personal style and turn ideas into finished work.

The original arts gallery preserves examples of Club activities. Contact staff to ask about current sessions and performances.

Making something of your own

Artworks activities described by the Club include beadwork, wood-burning, modeling, fashion, drama and music. These offer different ways for participants to work with materials, practice a skill and share creative work.

This is the range described in the Club's published overview. Confirm the current projects, supervision and schedule with staff.
