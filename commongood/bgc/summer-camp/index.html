2024 camp archive

The original announcement described 11 weeks from June 10 to August 23, 2024, weekdays 9 am–4:45 pm, with July 4 closed. Member registration began March 4; public registration began April 1. The announcement required scheduled visits, membership and summer forms, and payment.

The stated 2024 pricing was $80 for new or expired memberships; existing-member fees varied by membership date. The 100 available places and cash-or-money-order instructions were specific to that announcement.

For questions, the original contacts are 610-703-8575 and 484-239-2075.

Summers with something to discover

Future Stars summer camp brings friendships, activities and learning into the school break. Summer Brain Gain and reading activities help young people keep building their skills while making time for fun.
