Colonial Louisiana and New Orleans

French and then Spanish colonial rule shaped New Orleans amid slavery, Indigenous diplomacy, and Atlantic trade.

Historical setting in Liberation · the history below is factual, not the game’s plot

01 / The story

A city under changing flags

France ceded Louisiana to Spain in the 1760s, though transfer and local acceptance took time. New Orleans connected river networks to Caribbean commerce. Colonial authorities had ambitions, but their reach depended on local actors and negotiations.

02 / The story

Freedom and bondage

Enslaved Africans and their descendants were central to the economy and cultural life. Free people of color occupied a different legal position, but freedom did not erase racial restrictions. People pursued family, work, resistance, and autonomy within unequal rules.

03 / The story

Indigenous presence

The lower Mississippi was not empty land awaiting European government. Indigenous nations continued to trade, negotiate, resist, and adapt. Understanding this period requires seeing overlapping sovereignties rather than only a change in European flags.

The longer viewLegal status, race, and empire shaped lives differently within the same city.
