The chronological shelf

Choose an era; see the larger world.

The period links reflect the main historical settings of the games. A title locates a window in time; it is not a claim that the game’s plot happened.

431–404 BCE

Classical Greece in wartime

The Peloponnesian War pitted Athens and its maritime empire against Sparta and its allies, but “Greece” was no single state.

Odyssey ↗49–30 BCE

Egypt between dynasties and empire

In the final decades of the Ptolemaic kingdom, Egypt was wealthy and politically contested as Rome expanded its influence.

Origins ↗9th century

Baghdad at a crossroads

Abbasid Baghdad was a capital of administration, trade, scholarship, and religious life in a connected Afro-Eurasian world.

Mirage ↗late 9th century

Viking-age England

Raids, settlement, trade, and political negotiation connected Scandinavian groups to England’s changing kingdoms.

Valhalla ↗1189–1192

The Third Crusade and the Levant

Crusader and Muslim powers fought for cities and routes in a region already home to diverse communities.

Assassin’s Creed ↗late 15th century

Florence and Venice in the Renaissance

Florence and Venice were wealthy, competitive cities whose art and learning grew within complex political and commercial systems.

Assassin’s Creed II ↗early 16th century

Renaissance Rome

Popes, patrons, artists, diplomats, and armies remade Rome as both a spiritual center and a political capital.

Brotherhood ↗early 16th century

Ottoman Constantinople

After 1453, Constantinople became the Ottoman capital: an imperial city linking the Black Sea, Mediterranean, and inland routes.

Revelations ↗1570s–1580s

Japan at the end of the Sengoku era

Regional warfare and political consolidation reshaped Japan during the Azuchi–Momoyama transition.

Shadows ↗c. 1715–1722

The Caribbean age of piracy

Piracy thrived around commercial shipping and imperial rivalry, and those ships moved through an economy built partly on enslaved labor.

Black Flag ↗1754–1763

The Seven Years’ War

A struggle over territory and trade in North America became part of a wider global war among empires.

Rogue ↗1760s–1770s

Colonial Louisiana and New Orleans

French and then Spanish colonial rule shaped New Orleans amid slavery, Indigenous diplomacy, and Atlantic trade.

Liberation ↗1763–1783

The American Revolution

Colonial protest against British rule became a war for independence, while the meaning of liberty remained deeply contested.

Assassin’s Creed III ↗1789–1799

The French Revolution

Financial crisis and unequal privilege sparked a revolution that remade sovereignty, law, and political participation.

Unity ↗1868

Industrial London

Victorian London grew through industry, finance, and empire while exposing stark differences in wealth, work, and health.
